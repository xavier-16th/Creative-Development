The return of the emotion

A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away...

Anger have taken over the universe.

The Jedi Love and Desire, once the guardians of peace and justice, now find themselves in a battle against overwhelming feelings. As the dark side of emotions rises, the Jedi must confront their own inner turmoil to restore balance to the Force.

Master Love

Master Love is a wise and compassionate Jedi Knight who believes in the power of love to heal and unite. With a heart full of empathy, Master Love uses the Force to spread kindness and understanding throughout the galaxy. Their lightsaber, glowing with a soft pink hue, symbolizes the gentle yet powerful nature of love. Master Love's mission is to remind others that even in the darkest times, love can be a guiding light.

Padawan Desire

Padawan Desire is a young and passionate Jedi apprentice who is still learning to control their emotions. They are driven by a deep longing for connection and understanding, which sometimes leads them down a dangerous path. Padawan Desire's lightsaber glows with a fiery red hue, reflecting the intensity of their emotions. Despite their struggles, Padawan Desire is determined to find balance within themselves and harness the power of desire for good, hoping to one day become a powerful Jedi Knight like Master Love.
